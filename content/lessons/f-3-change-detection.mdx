<Article id="why" title="Why this matters for your self-model">

Before a reviewer accepts a learned body-change detector, they'll ask how it compares to CUSUM on the residual of a simple model. That's a fair question, and after Month 1 (residuals, sliding-window least squares) and Month 2 (why amplitude scores are sign-ambiguous), you can answer it with numbers. CUSUM accumulates evidence of a mean shift and trades detection delay against false-alarm rate explicitly; GLR also estimates *when* the change happened and how big it was.

</Article>
