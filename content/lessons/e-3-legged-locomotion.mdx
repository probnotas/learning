<Article id="why" title="Why this matters for your self-model">

The spring-loaded inverted pendulum (SLIP) is a compliant pendulum that hops. Templates and anchors is the idea that a simple model (a template) can be embedded in a complex body (an anchor). That's a self-model philosophy in its own right.
