<Article id="why" title="Why this matters for your self-model">

This is the formal version of everything Feynman showed you. $I\ddot\theta + c\dot\theta + \kappa\theta = k\theta_m(t)$ is a constant-coefficient second-order linear ODE, and 18.03 Unit II solves it completely: characteristic roots (your eigenvalues), under-, critically and over-damped regimes, the exponential response formula (your transfer function), gain and phase lag, and *practical* resonance (the true peak, which sits slightly below $\omega_n$ when damping is non-zero). It's the month where resonance becomes visible in your own code, through the Compliant Pendulum Playground.

</Article>
