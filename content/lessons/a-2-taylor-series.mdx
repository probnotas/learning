## Why this matters

Every "linearized" model you've used, $\sin\theta \approx \theta$, $A_d \approx I + \Delta t A$, $e^{\lambda\Delta t} \approx 1 + \lambda\Delta t$, is the first term of a Taylor series. The next term tells you how wrong it is and when it breaks: the $-\theta^3/6$ term is why your pendulum's resonance softens at large swings, and the $\Delta t^2$ term is why explicit Euler gains energy. Self-models that extrapolate beyond their training data fail exactly where the neglected terms grow.
