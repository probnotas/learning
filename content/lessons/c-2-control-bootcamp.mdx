## Why this matters

Control is where a self-model earns its keep. An LQR controller is designed *from* a model of the body, and a Kalman filter *is* a running self-model that fuses predictions with measurements. Its innovation (prediction error) is the classical fault-detection signal. Brunton's bootcamp is written in exactly the linear-algebra and eigenvalue language from Month 1, so you can now follow it on your own terms.
