<Article id="why" title="Why this matters for your self-model">

One camp of the work you're entering learns dynamics end-to-end from pixels and actions; the other (F2) models the body explicitly. To defend your work in a 1X World Model Lab conversation you need to explain how each approach would have handled the mass bump. A learned latent dynamics model sees it as distribution shift in its prediction error. A self-model sees it as a parameter change. You also need to say what each one costs.

</Article>
