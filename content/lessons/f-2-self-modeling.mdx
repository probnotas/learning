<Article id="why" title="Why this matters for your self-model">

This is your home literature. Bongard, Zykov & Lipson (2006) showed a robot inferring its own morphology through self-directed experiments and recovering from damage. Cully et al. (2015) showed fast adaptation by searching a precomputed behaviour map. Both are, at heart, the *excitation + identification + residual* loop you built in Month 1 (least squares) and Month 3 (sensitivities), just at much larger scale.

</Article>
