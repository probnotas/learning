## Why this matters

Month 5's control bootcamp assumes you can read a transfer function and its poles. The Laplace transform is the tool that turns your pendulum's ODE (including start-up transients and a sudden mass bump) into algebra, and the poles of $H(s)$ are the eigenvalues you met in Month 1. It also handles step inputs and impulses, which is how you'd model "the body changed at time $t_0$".
